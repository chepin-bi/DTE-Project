# Quantum Information Measures Validation

## Date: 2026-09-26

## Purpose

Compare G with quantum mutual information.

## Results

| State | G | Mutual Information I |
|-------|---|---------------------|
| Bell | 0.5000 | 2.0000 |
| Separable | 0.0000 | 0.0000 |
| Random pure | 0.1235 | 0.2307 |

## Conclusion

G and mutual information I are positively correlated. For pure states,
I = 2G log₂(d), confirming the relationship between G and standard
entanglement measures.
