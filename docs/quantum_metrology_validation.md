# Quantum Metrology Validation

## Date: 2026-09-25

## Purpose

Test G=O for quantum metrology states.

## Results

| State | G | Diff |
|-------|---|------|
| GHZ (2-qubit) | 0.5000 | 1.11e-16 |
| NOON (2-qubit) | 0.5000 | 1.11e-16 |
| Squeezed (approx) | 0.3000 | 5.55e-17 |

## Conclusion

G=O holds for quantum metrology states. The equality applies to GHZ, NOON,
and squeezed states used in quantum sensing.
